Privacy Policy

Fruit Merge · Effective 19 November 2026

Privacy Terms of Use

This policy explains how Fruit Merge (“the app”) handles information. The app is a casual game. It does not ask you to create an account.

Information stored on your device

The app saves game progress on your device only. That includes your best score, level progress, statistics, achievements, booster inventory, daily reward streak, tutorial progress, and settings such as sound, music, haptics, graphics, and accessibility options. This information stays on the device and is not sent to us.

Android backup may include this app data if backup is enabled on your device. You can clear it by clearing the app’s storage or uninstalling the app.

Advertising

The app shows ads through Google AdMob, including a banner at the bottom of the screen and a full-screen ad when a game ends. To deliver and measure ads, Google may collect device identifiers such as the advertising ID, IP address, and information about the device and ad interactions.

Google’s use of that information is described in the Google Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services. You can limit personalized ads in your device settings under Google ads settings, or reset your advertising ID.

Permissions

  • Internet and network state, used to load ads.
  • Vibration, used for optional haptic feedback while you play.

The app does not request your location, contacts, photos, microphone, or camera.

Children

Fruit Merge is a general-audience game. It is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
